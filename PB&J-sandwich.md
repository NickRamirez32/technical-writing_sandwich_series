# Making a Peanut Butter and Jelly Sandwich

## Overview

A peanut butter and jelly sandwich (PB&J) is a simple American snack made from sliced bread, peanut butter, and fruit jelly or preserves. 

## Audience and use cases

This procedure is intended for beginners who need clear, step-by-step instructions for making a peanut butter and jelly sandwich. It is useful for teaching basic cooking skills to children or new cooks. 

## Prerequisites

### Ingredients

- Sliced bread
- Peanut butter
- Jelly or preserves

### Materials

- Plate or napkin
- Butter knife
  
> Caution: Use the butter knife carefully to avoid cuts.

## Pre-task Checklist

- [ ] Gather all ingredients and materials
- [ ] Prepare a clean, safe work surface
- [ ] Wash your hands

> Warning: Check for peanut or food allergies before consuming or serving to others.

## Steps

1. Place two slices of bread on a plate or napkin.
2. Use a butter knife to spread a layer of peanut butter on one slice of bread until the surface is evenly coated. 
3. Spread jelly or preserves on the second slice of bread until the surface is evenly coated. 
4. Press the slices together so the peanut butter and jelly face each other.

> Tip: Rinse your knife after you spread your peanut butter to avoid cross-contamination.

## Variations

| Type | Change | Notes |
|:------|:--------|:------|
| Crunchy PB | Use crunchy peanut butter | Adds texture |
| Honey | Replace jelly or preserves with honey | Sweet and salty |
| No sugar added | Use natural peanut butter and 100% fruit spread | Less sweet option |
| Whole wheat or grain | Use whole wheat or grain bread | More fiber |
| Toasted | Toast bread before adding peanut butter and jelly | Richer flavor |
| Grilled | Grill sandwich in a buttered skillet | Buttery, toasted flavor |

> Note: Toasted or grilled peanut butter and jelly sandwiches are advanced techniques. For more information on advanced techniques, visit [Advanced PB&J](https://www.example.com/...).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible cause | Resolution |
|:--------|:---------------|:-----------|
| Sandwich is falling apart | Too much jelly or peanut butter | Use thinner layers on each slice of bread |
| Bread is cracking or ripping | Bread is too old or dry | Use fresher, slightly firmer bread |
| Sandwich is too dry | Using too little jelly or peanut butter | Increase the amount of jelly or peanut butter |
