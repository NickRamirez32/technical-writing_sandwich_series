# Making a Grilled Cheese Sandwich

## Overview

A grilled cheese sandwich is an American snack made from sliced bread and processed cheese. It is cooked until the cheese melts and the outside is a golden brown.

## Audience and use cases

This procedure is for beginners who need clear, step-by-step instructions for making a grilled cheese sandwich. It is useful for teaching foundational cooking skills to children or new cooks.

## Prerequisites

### Ingredients

- Sliced bread
- Sliced cheese (for example, American)
- Butter

### Materials

- Cast iron skillet
- Butter knife
- Plate
- Wooden spatula
- Kitchen knife
- Cutting board
- Cleaning cloth  (100% lint-free cotton)
- Oven mitt

> Note: Cast iron requires special care. See **Cast Iron Care Guide** in **See Also** section below. 

## Pre-task checklist 

- [ ] Gather ingredients and materials
- [ ] Prepare a clean, safe workspace
- [ ] Wash your hands

## Steps

1. Pre-heat cast iron skillet on low-medium to medium heat for 3-5 minutes.
   
    > Caution: The cast iron skillet and the stove top will be hot. Always use an oven mitt when handling hot cast iron. 

2. Coat the cast iron skillet in a **thin** layer of butter.
3. Use the butter knife and apply a thin layer of butter to one side of bread.
   
   > Caution: Use the butter knife carefully to avoid cuts.

4. Place your bread butter-side down into the cast iron skillet.
5. Set one slice of American cheese on top of the bread in the cast iron skillet.
   
   > Note: Remove any plastic wrapping from individually wrapped sliced cheese.

6. Use the butter knife and apply a thin layer of butter to one side of the second slice of bread.
7. Place the second slice of bread, butter side up, on the cheese and bread in the cast iron skillet.
8. Flip the sandwich carefully using the spatula.
9.  Apply firm pressure to the grilled cheese sandwich with the back of the spatula.
10. Turn off the stove and remove cast iron skillet from heat.
    
    > Warning: Never leave hot pans unattended.

11. Transfer grilled cheese to cutting board using the wooden spatula .
12. Use the kitchen knife to cut the grilled cheese sandwich at a 45 degree angle to create two equally sized triangles. 

> Caution: Use the kitchen knife carefully to avoid cuts.

13. Transfer both halves of the grilled cheese to a plate and serve. 

> Caution: Contents will be hot. Enjoy carefully.

## Variations
 

| Type | Change | Notes |
| :--- | :---   | :---  |
| Extra cheese | Add an extra slice of cheese | Cheesier flavor |
| Mayonnaise | Swap butter for mayonnaise | Easier spread and more even cooking |
| Sourdough | Replace standard bread with sliced sourdough | Crunchy texture |
| Ham and cheese | Add cooked and sliced ham | Savory, rich flavor |
| Artisan bread | Replace sliced bread with artisanal bread | Flavors and textures change |
| Artisan cheese | Replace sliced cheese with artisanal cheeses | Melt and flavors change |

> Note: Cooking times may need to be adjusted if using a variation.

## Extra tips

If you are making multiple grilled cheese sandwiches, use the cleaning cloth to clean out the skillet between sandwiches.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible cause | Resolution |
| :---    | :---           | :---       |
| Foul smell | Butter or bread is burning | Reduce heat (see step 1 for heat settings) |
| Bread is breaking | Bread is too old | Use fresher, slightly firmer bread |
| Sandwich is too dry | Not enough cheese | Add an extra slice of cheese |
| Still hungry | Did not make enough | Make another grilled cheese sandwich |
